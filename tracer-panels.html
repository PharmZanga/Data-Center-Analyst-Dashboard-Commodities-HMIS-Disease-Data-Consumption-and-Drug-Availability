    Facility and district support priorities

    Transparent triage score: 60 × zero-stock record share + 40 × records with under 30 days cover share. A previously observed unit missing from the latest snapshot is assigned 100 for reporting follow-up. Scores compare stock/reporting evidence only; disease burden and actual report timeliness are unavailable. Counts refer to reporting units.

    Projected stock cover and repeat zero-stock observations

    Ranked by estimated days of cover from the report date. Zero-stock observations are snapshot counts; continuous duration and separate episodes cannot be established from weekly gaps. A projected date in the past is not evidence that a stockout happened.

    Redistribution candidates · review required

    Same district, exact item and programme; named nonaggregate units only. Sources exceed 4 MOS and retain at least 2 months of reported AMC after all proposed allocations. Destinations receive up to one month of reported AMC. Pack sizes, transport, expiry, programme restrictions and current stock require human validation.

    Data validation

    District reporting coverage history

    Reported units / union of units observed across these three October weeks. This is observed coverage, not official completeness. Submission timestamps and due dates are missing, so timeliness cannot be calculated.

    Action log · saved in this browser

    Local to this browser, not a shared multi-user system. Export regularly. Weekly briefs are downloadable for review and sharing; automatic email/WhatsApp delivery is not configured.

    Measures awaiting data

    • Case-linked expected consumption, malaria RDT positivity and ACT alignment: needs actual cases, tests and dispensing, mapped by commodity and district.
    • Seasonal outbreak thresholds and year-on-year comparisons: needs comparable historical weekly disease series and reporting coverage. No prior-year epidemic threshold is fabricated.
    • Expiry/near-expiry: needs batch expiry dates and batch balances. Overstock is currently classified using reported MOS.
    • Order fill rate, lead times and emergency orders: needs request, approval, dispatch and delivery records.
    • Exact stockout duration/episodes and report timeliness: needs daily stock events, submission timestamps and reporting deadlines.

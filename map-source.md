# Zambia province boundaries

Source: Zambia Data Hub, distributed by geoBoundaries gbOpen.
Boundary ID: ZMB-ADM1-6923714; reference year: 2020; 10 provinces.

Metadata: https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/ZMB/ADM1/

The original project GeoJSON is retained as zambia-provinces.geojson. build-map.mjs simplifies the outlines at 0.012 degree tolerance and projects them to a local equirectangular SVG centred at latitude 13.5°S. Province labels use manually positioned points. These generalized shapes are for dashboard visualization, not surveying or legal boundary decisions. The displayed indicators remain simulated data.

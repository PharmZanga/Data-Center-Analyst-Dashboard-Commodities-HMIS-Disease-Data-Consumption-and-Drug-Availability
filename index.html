        Data notes and operating guide

        Select a province on the map, then a district or facility to drill down. All five views use the selected location and period. Stock metrics use the final selected month; trends use the selected window. Province maps retain national context. Download data dictionary and guide · Stakeholder insights

        Months of stock = stock on hand ÷ average consumption over the latest 3 months. Stockout = zero stock; low = positive stock below 2 months; adequate = 2+ months. Availability = share of facility–tracer pairs with positive stock. Reporting completeness and on-time reporting are shown separately. Disease counts are aggregate; no patient records are used. District labels and facilities are fictional. Province shapes use generalized 2020 geoBoundaries boundaries. Regression and forecasts describe sample data and are not clinical or procurement recommendations. Live feeds, scheduled refresh, email/SMS delivery and enforced analyst/viewer roles require a connected backend; they are not active in this offline prototype.

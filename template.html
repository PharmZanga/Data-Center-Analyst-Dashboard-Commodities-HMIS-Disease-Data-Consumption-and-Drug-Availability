    Anomalies

      Data notes

      Months of stock = stock on hand divided by average monthly consumption. Stockout = zero stock on the reporting day. Low = under 2 months. Availability index = share of tracer drugs in stock, averaged across facilities in a district. Reporting is on time if received by the 15th. HMIS counts are aggregate with no patient-level data, and usually lag 1 to 2 weeks. Anomaly rule: consumption per case more than 2 standard deviations above its 12-month average.
